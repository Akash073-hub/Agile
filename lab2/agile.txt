Write one example where a traditional software
development approach does not support this
value.
Write one example showing how Agile addresses

For each value:
Individuals and interactions over processes
and tools
Traditional limitation:
A company follows strict processes where all
communication must go through formal documents
and emails. Team members rarely talk directly.
Even small decisions need manager approval. This
slows problem-solving and reduces teamwork.
Agile approach:
Agile teams use daily stand-ups and direct
conversations. Developers, testers, and designers
talk openly to solve issues quickly. Collaboration is
encouraged over rigid procedures.
Working software over comprehensive
documentation
Traditional limitation:
A team spends months writing detailed documentation
before coding starts. By the time software is built,
requirements may have changed, and the product may not
meet user needs.
Agile approach:
Agile teams build small working features in short sprints.
They show real software frequently to stakeholders.
Documentation exists, but working software is the priority.
Customer collaboration over contract
negotiation
Traditional limitation:
Once a contract is signed, changes are discouraged. If the
customer wants modifications, it requires renegotiation,
delays, and extra costs. The product may not fully satisfy the
customer.
Agile approach:
Customers are involved throughout development. They give
feedback every sprint. Changes are welcomed if they improve
the product, leading to higher satisfaction.
Responding to change over following a plan
Traditional limitation:
A project follows a fixed plan created at the start. Even if
market needs change, the team sticks to the original plan
to avoid delays. The final product may become outdated.
Agile approach:
Agile teams expect change. They adjust priorities each
sprint. This flexibility helps them deliver a product that
matches current needs.
